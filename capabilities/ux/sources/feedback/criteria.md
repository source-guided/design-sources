# Understanding outcomes

- What outcome or condition does the user need to understand?
- What is already conveyed, and what information remains missing?
- What does the information apply to, and how long must it remain available?
- Is the relationship between explanation, supporting detail and the next action clear?
- What is conveyed visually, semantically and through assistive access in the relevant context?
