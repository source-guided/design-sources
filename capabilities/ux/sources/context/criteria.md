# Task and design context

- What is the user trying to accomplish, and what do they already know?
- Which requirements and preferences came from the user or the product owner, and which choices remain open?
- Which information and actions have priority for this audience and situation?
- Which existing design or source is applicable, and what is its scope and authority?

[Supporting references](../../../../references/context/index.json) belong to this subject. Product-specific choices and evaluation cases remain with their product or task.
